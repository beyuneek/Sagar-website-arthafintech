Our specialisation

Property accounting built for property management companies.

Most accounting firms can read a P&L. Fewer understand the complexity behind a property portfolio.

At Artha Finedge, we provide outsourced property accounting support to property management companies, owners and investors. From rent rolls and CAM reconciliations to owner reporting, property closings and escrow accounting, we handle the detail behind the numbers — accurately, consistently and on time.

You manage the properties. We manage the accounting behind them.

See property accounting

Rent roll & lease tie-out

Rent roll agreed to the ledger every month, with abatements, escalations and straight-lining applied correctly.

Escrow, deposits & trust

Security deposits and escrow balances reconciled and segregated, so trust obligations hold up under audit.

CapEx vs repairs

A capitalization policy applied consistently. Maintain it, expense it. Improve it, capitalize it.

CAM & recoveries

Common area maintenance pools, tenant recovery calculations and year-end true-ups you can defend line by line.

Property closings

Settlement statements booked correctly at acquisition and disposal, with basis, prorations and loan costs in the right place.

Owner & investor reporting

Owner statements, distribution schedules and investor packs on a fixed date, in your format.

How we work

You see our work before you commit.

We start with a focused pilot on your actual work, so you can judge our accuracy, communication and turnaround before making a longer-term commitment.

  1. STEP 01

    Discovery Call

    We understand your business, current processes, systems, challenges and expectations. Together, we define the scope and identify where our team can make the biggest difference.

  2. STEP 02

    Two-Week Pilot

    We start with a focused pilot based on your actual work. You get to experience our processes, communication, accuracy and turnaround before making a longer-term commitment.

  3. STEP 03

    Transition

    Once you’re ready to move forward, we take care of the handover. We document processes, establish workflows, coordinate access and responsibilities, and ensure a smooth transfer to our team.

  4. STEP 04

    Steady State

    Your dedicated team takes ownership of the agreed scope, working to defined timelines and reporting standards. Regular reviews, clear communication and continuous process improvement keep everything running smoothly.
